Lab 01: APA Citations & Zotero

APA citation style and managing references with Zotero.

Preparation

Use a laptop with Zotero installed, if possible.

Lab Overview

Learning objectives

  1. Format APA-style references and in-text citations for common source types.

  2. Build and organize a Zotero library by adding items and editing their metadata.

  3. Generate APA-formatted citations and bibliographies from Zotero in your documents.
