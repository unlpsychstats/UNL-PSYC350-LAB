Between-Subjects One-Way ANOVA One categorical IV with 2 levels (2 separate groups of people) One continuous dependent variable Do students who vote in the ASUN elections have a higher GPA on average than those who do not vote in school elections? Do Nebraska football fans tend to drink more alcohol on average during game days when the Huskers win compared to when they lose?
