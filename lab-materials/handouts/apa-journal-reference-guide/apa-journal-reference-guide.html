Narrative and parenthetical citations always have the same form (Name, Publication Year)

Reference List Notes:

  • “References” heading: bolded and centered
  • Everything double-spaced and no extra spaces between heading and first reference
  • Indent second/subsequent lines of each reference by a .5 in hanging indent (“tab” on keyboard)
  • Alphabetize by first author’s last name
  • When a journal article has a DOI, present it as a URL in the format https://doi.org/10.xxxx/xxxxx.

ALSO: Alphabetize multiple in-text citations within same set of parentheses; separate with semi-colon

Journal Article Reference List Checklist (For EACH source)

1. Author(s)

  • Names in same order as listed on the article title page
  • Author’s last name, followed by a comma, and their initials with a period and a space after each initial–NO full first or middle names
  • Comma after final initial of previous author and before last name of the next author
  • “&” before final author’s last name
  • If 20 or more authors, list first 20 authors, then “ … ”, then last author
  • Period after final author’s initials

3. Article Title

  • Use sentence case–Lowercase most words; only capitalize the first word of the title, proper nouns, and the first word after punctuation within the title (e.g., after a colon)
  • No italics or any other special formatting
  • Period at the end of title

4. Journal Title

  • Write the title as shown on the cited work
  • Use Title Case–Capitalize most words; only lowercase words of three letters or fewer (except the first word in the title and after punctuation within the title)
  • Italicize the journal title
  • Comma after the end of title; do not italicize the comma

2. Year (xxxx)

  • In parentheses
  • Year only (other periodicals [e.g., blog, newspaper, magazine] may include month, day, or season)
  • Period after closing parenthesis

5. Journal Volume Number

  • Italicize the volume
  • If no issue number, place a comma directly after the number
  • Do not italicize the comma after the volume number

6. Journal Issue Number

  • Include the issue number whenever available
  • Put the number in parentheses DIRECTLY after the volume number–no space between the volume number and the opening parentheses
  • Do not use italics or any other special formatting for this number and the parentheses
  • Comma after closing parenthesis

7. Page Numbers

  • Include full page range of article, even if you only use information from one page
  • No italics or any other special formatting
  • Separate the page numbers in the range using an en dash “–“ (this is longer than a hyphen, and is usually typed by hitting option+hyphen, or alt+hyphen)
  • No page abbreviation such as pp. or pg. before the page numbers
  • Period after second page number
  • If an article number or eLocator instead of a page range, write the word “Article” (capitalized) and then the article number

8. DOI or URL

  • Include the DOI if available
  • Use the CrossRef DOI search engine if you cannot find the DOI in your article
  • Include the URL (website address) if retrieved online and it has no DOI
  • Copy and paste the EXACT string of letters and numbers
  • NO closing punctuation
  • Do not write “Retrieved from”
